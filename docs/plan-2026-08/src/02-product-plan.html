PRODUCT / POSITIONING

What CryptAI is — and the three promises it can keep

An AI trading co-pilot for crypto perpetual futures. The user brings an exchange account (or a paper desk); CryptAI brings a validated market-condition engine, a metered session where AI agents propose trades, and an unmetered monitor that manages every open position.

Promise 1 · Timing

"Is now a good time to trade?"

Answered by the Rust signal engine's tradability score — empirically validated as a short-horizon filter (IC +0.08–0.10), not vibes. Surfaced as a hot-window banner: spend your session slots when the market is actually tradable.

Promise 2 · Personal

"A trade team working your book."

Setups are synthesized per user — same chart, different trade — sized against your capital, risk appetite, monthly goal, and open positions. Every trade arrives as a proposal with entry, stop, targets, size, and a plain-language rationale. You approve; it executes.

Promise 3 · Vigilance

"It doesn't fall asleep after entry."

On fill, a per-position monitor agent takes over — unmetered, outliving the session and every restart. It re-checks the thesis as conditions drift and decides: hold, tighten stop, take partial, or exit. Finding trades is metered; protecting them never is.

The one-line pitch

"Up to two 30-minute sessions a day with a professional AI trade desk — a slot is only spent when it delivers you a trade, and an agent watches every position you take, around the clock, for free."

On approval the trade executes and the session goes synchronous: no new setups. The user's whole window pivots to the one running trade.

→
5

Managed exit

The monitor manages the position to profit-book or stop — even after the session ends. Portfolio and history update; the next session unlocks when the trade closes.

Why sessions, not always-on

  • Scarcity is the product: session slots make the free tier generous but bounded, and the upgrade path obvious.
  • Cost follows sessions: LLM spend only happens inside a metered window — the $0 infra budget holds structurally.
  • Focus beats noise: a 30-min window with a hot-market banner beats a firehose of all-day signals.

Why synchronous on free

  • One open trade at a time = capital protection by design for beginners.
  • Monitoring one trade is cheap and unmetered — the "never sleeps" promise costs us almost nothing.
  • Async multi-trade becomes a real, felt upgrade at Lite/Pro instead of an arbitrary paywall.

Session rules (free) — v2 after red-team review

  • Up to 2 slots/day · 30 min each · server-authoritative clock · daily reset at midnight (stored UTC, always shown in the user's local time).
  • A slot is spent when a proposal is delivered — not when scanning starts. A session that finds no qualifying setup auto-ends after a bounded scan (~10 min) and returns the slot; scan attempts cap at 3/day to protect the request budget.
  • Clock pauses while a proposal awaits a decision.
  • While a trade is open, new sessions are held; a slot held by an open trade survives one midnight reset (intraday holds run 4–12h — the lock routinely spans midnight).

PRODUCT / QUANT DESK — WHAT WE CLAIM, WHAT WE NEVER CLAIM

The honest edge — claims the data supports

We claim — and can defend

  • "Now is / isn't a good time to trade." Tradability score validated out-of-sample as a short-horizon filter (IC +0.083–0.096 at 4 bars; survives volatility normalization; re-validated on six held-out symbols).
  • "This setup fits your book." Sizing and gating are deterministic against the user's real capital, risk %, and open positions.
  • "Your position is being watched." Monitor re-evaluates on regime flip, pulse veto, and invalidation breach — mechanical, auditable triggers.

We never claim

  • Direction from regime. Killed out-of-sample (walk-forward IC −0.008). No consumer may map regime → long/short. Contract-tested.
  • "3 PM will be the best hour." The score is a now-cast, not a forecast. The hot-window banner says "market is hot now" and "typically active hours" — never a predicted golden hour, until logged calibration data earns it.
  • Guaranteed returns. Monthly range is the user's goal input for risk shaping — never a promise in UI copy.

Launch posture: paper-first

Waitlist launch runs on paper desks (real fills simulation) + Delta India testnet connect. Real-money mainnet stays double-gated off until the compliance floor exists (ToS, risk disclosure, automated-execution consent, API-key scope verification, KYC). Rationale:

  • 50 strangers × leveraged perps × an engine with a brand-new wiring layer = uninsurable risk on day one.
  • Paper mode exercises 100% of the product loop — sessions, proposals, focus mode, monitoring, history.
  • India: FIU-IND / VDA / advisory-licensing questions are open; paper keeps them dormant while we learn.

Falsifiability from day one

  • Every published pulse is appended to the pulse_snapshots calibration log — the score stays testable against forward returns.
  • Every proposal → outcome pair is recorded, so "did our setups make money" is a query, not a belief.
  • Hot-window hours are learned from our own logged data before we ever present them as advice.

PRODUCT / LAUNCH SCOPE

Waitlist launch: must / should / won't

Must — launch blocks on these

  • Google sign-in + 4-step onboarding (mode → capital → risk appetite → monthly risk budget — never a return expectation)
  • Hot-window banner + per-symbol pulse on dashboard
  • 2×30-min session flow: start → setups → proposal card → approve → trade focus
  • Paper execution + portfolio/history that survives restarts
  • Per-position monitor + visible monitor window ("what my agent is doing")
  • Quota integrity: server-clamped sessions, per-session LLM budget fed & enforced
  • Security floor: admin fail-closed, no fail-open anon mode, destructive scripts out of the image

Should — first weeks after

  • Delta India testnet live-connect beta (real order path, fake money)
  • Learned "typically hot hours" from calibration log
  • Session summary recap ("your 30 minutes: 3 setups, 1 trade, +$142")
  • Email/push nudge when the market turns hot and slots remain
  • Waitlist referral loop ("skip the queue")

Won't — deliberately not now

  • Payments, paid tiers, checkout — Free beta only
  • Real-money mainnet execution — gated until compliance floor
  • Scalp channel — TDS honesty + validation gap
  • AI chat assistant — Lite/Pro roadmap
  • CoinDCX — no testnet exists; unvalidatable without real money
  • Mobile app — responsive web only
